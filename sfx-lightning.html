Lightning — Built By Hand

The last cue. Four layered mixes.

30 of 31 locked. This is the only one left.

Three rounds of asking a model for "lightning" all failed the same way — it returns one sound, either a buzz or a rumble. Real game lightning is at least two layers: a sharp electrical crack and a deep thunder behind it. So I generated the crack, the thunder and a sizzle separately and mixed them myself, varying the balance and the delay between the crack and the boom.
